Location

Location

Mbano Manor Hotel Victoria Falls is located on the Old Kazungula Road, only 4 kilometres from the entrance to the Victoria Falls Park. Access to Victoria Falls is by road, air or rail. Scheduled flights connect Victoria Falls with local and regional destinations.

What You Need to Know

Hotel check-in and checkout times

Check-in is from 14:00 and checkout on the day of departure is 11:00. Guests are welcome to arrive at Mbano Manor Hotel earlier, and make use of our swimming pool and garden. We try to accommodate early arrivals or late checkouts where possible, subject to room availability.
