Victoria Falls

Experience the Majestic Victoria Falls!

A World Heritage Site and one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World.

Mosi-oa-Tunya

The Smoke That Thunders

The enduring appeal of the Victoria Falls is unrivalled in global and African tourism, offering nostalgia, environmental conservation and geological fascination. The Victoria Falls is the largest waterfall in the world, created continuously as the mighty Zambezi River makes a sudden kilometre wide drop and plunges over 100m into a gorge. Two national parks, Victoria Falls National Park and Zambezi National Park, adjoin the Falls. The local name for Victoria Falls is Mosi-oa-Tunya, the Smoke that Thunders.

Zambezi National Park

Game drive with an experienced guide

Game drives at Victoria Falls set off either in the early morning or mid-afternoon, the open four-wheel drive vehicle allows you to discover many of the African bush and its inhabitants. Qualified guides are on hand to explain this adventure.

An African Safari Awaits You!

You are spoilt for choice

The Chamabonda National Park is a sub section of the Zambezi National Park. A vast plain that shows evidence of a long massive river teeming with wild animals. Hwange National Park, the largest of Zimbabwe's national parks, is easily accessible by road or air, offering an extended safari experience. Hwange National Park has one of the largest elephant populations in the world and is renowned for massive herds of wild game, including Africa's big five. Alternatively, a thrilling Chobe day trip from Victoria Falls. Situated only one hour's drive from Victoria Falls, the Chobe National Park in Botswana boasts one of the largest wildlife populations in Africa, not to mention the magnificent Chobe River.

Set sail on the great Zambezi

Zambezi River Boat Cruise

Sail along the Zambezi River. Elephants, hippos, crocodile and a wide range of African bird life can be seen on land, in the air and the water. Learn from a knowledgeable guide as the sun sets in this spectacular environment or book a picnic lunch or dinner cruise.

The thrill of the adrenaline rush

Victoria Falls Adventures

An exhilarating adventure brings a different experience of the Victoria Falls. Experience magnificent scenery and rapids of the mighty Zambezi river, one of the great rafting adventures in Africa. From abseiling across the Zambezi gorge to visiting the elephant sanctuary, you are spoilt for choice!

Fly with the angels

View from the Sky

Experience the true wonder of the Victoria Falls from a bird's eye view. Marvel at the zig-zagging funnel of gorges that the plunging sheets of water spray into.
